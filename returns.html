Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Urban Store Shoes, we want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If you are not completely satisfied with your shoes, you may be eligible to return them in accordance with the conditions below.

1. Return Window

We accept eligible returns within 30 days of delivery.

To qualify for a return, the shoes must:

  • Be unworn
  • Be in their original condition
  • Include the original shoe box and packaging
  • Include original tags and accessories, where applicable
  • Not show signs of outdoor use, damage, or alteration

Items returned after the 30-day return period may not be accepted.

2. Shoe-Specific Return Conditions

Because footwear is generally considered a product that can be tried for size and fit, we recommend trying shoes indoors on a clean, carpeted or otherwise protected surface.

Shoes may not qualify for a standard return if they show:

  • Dirt or mud
  • Scuffed soles
  • Scratches or visible damage
  • Outdoor wear
  • Creasing caused by use
  • Stains
  • Pet hair or other debris
  • Missing or damaged original packaging
  • Signs of washing or improper cleaning

The shoe box should also be returned in reasonable condition. Please do not place shipping labels directly on the original shoe box where possible.

3. Size and Fit

If the shoes do not fit as expected, you may request a return or exchange within the applicable return period, provided the shoes meet the return conditions.

Please refer to the size chart on the relevant product page before ordering.

Because fit can vary between different shoe styles, we cannot guarantee that the same size will fit identically across every product.

4. How to Request a Return

Before sending an item back, please contact us at:

Email: hello@urbanstore.com

Please include:

  • Order number
  • Name used for the order
  • Product you wish to return
  • Reason for the return
  • Photographs, if the product is damaged or defective

We will provide return instructions after reviewing your request.

Please do not send a return to an address without receiving return instructions from us.

5. Return Shipping

For a standard change-of-mind or size-related return, the customer may be responsible for the cost of return shipping unless otherwise stated at checkout or required by applicable law.

If the shoes are:

  • Defective
  • Damaged during delivery
  • Incorrectly shipped

we will review the issue and may provide an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement, exchange, or refund.

6. Damaged or Incorrect Shoes

Please contact us as soon as possible if your package arrives damaged or you receive the wrong product or size.

We may request photographs of:

  • The shoes
  • The soles
  • The product packaging
  • The shipping package
  • The shipping label

This information helps us investigate the issue.

7. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect an eligible returned item, we will notify you whether the return has been approved.

If approved, the refund will generally be issued to the original payment method.

Refund processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

Original shipping charges may not be refundable unless the return is due to an error, defect, damage, or another circumstance where a refund is required by applicable law.

8. Exchanges

If you would like to exchange your shoes for a different size, please contact us before returning the item.

Size exchanges are subject to product availability.

If the requested size is unavailable, we may offer a refund or another suitable option.

9. Sale and Promotional Items

Sale or promotional shoes may be subject to different return conditions.

Any special return restrictions will be clearly communicated on the relevant product page or at checkout.

Nothing in this section limits any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

10. Defective Products

If you believe your shoes have a manufacturing defect, please contact us with your order information and photographs showing the issue.

Normal wear and tear, accidental damage, improper care, or damage caused by use outside the intended purpose may not qualify as a manufacturing defect, subject to applicable law.

11. Lost or Undelivered Returns

Customers are responsible for ensuring that returned packages are properly packaged and shipped to the address provided by Urban Store Shoes.

We recommend using a shipping service that provides tracking and proof of delivery.

We may not be able to process a refund for a return that does not reach us.

12. Consumer Rights

This policy does not affect any mandatory consumer rights available under applicable law.

Customers in the United States, United Kingdom, and other jurisdictions may have statutory rights regarding defective products, cancellations, refunds, exchanges, and other consumer protections.

Where applicable law provides greater rights than this policy, the applicable law will take precedence.

13. Changes to This Policy

Urban Store Shoes may update this Return & Refund Policy from time to time.

The latest version will be posted on this page with the updated Last Updated date.

14. Contact Us

For return, exchange, or refund questions, please contact:

Urban Store Shoes
Email: hello@urbanstore.com
